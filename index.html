OneBISS I&S Spine Map, MYP1–5

A proposed merged spine of 20 units (four per year, two per term), built from the Sentrum sequence of September 2026, the Gausel I&S subject overview, the Gausel MYP4–5 planners in Toddle, the current Gausel MYP2 list, and the notes from our collaboration session. Each open question is a choice on the left. Pick an option and the spine and coverage checks on the right update to match.

Bottom line: once units are paired by concept, the two schools already line up in almost every slot. The real choices are where governance lives in MYP1, whether MYP2 ends with Refugees or Sustainable Energy, and how much of Fringes (now an optional last unit in MYP5) moves into the MYP4 hate unit so every student meets it. Four strands run up the five years: power and governance, people and planet, identity and belonging, and change and innovation. The recommended path (loaded now) assesses every criterion once per term, uses all six global contexts, and gives revolutions, WWI and climate one clear home each.

Where revolutions and WWI sit

Revolutions currently turn up in eight places across both schools. The proposal gives the Industrial Revolution one owner (MYP3) and keeps "innovation and revolution" as a concept everywhere else.

Where climate and human impact sit

Biomes, the carbon cycle, deforestation and climate change are spread so each year meets them through a different lens: ecological systems, local tourism, technology, cities, then cause and effect.

Before anything is final

  • Transfer every unit into the 2027–28 Toddle, including units this map folds or retires (Charismatic Leaders, Vanishing Points, Fair Access & HDI, Plastics). Hide them from parents until the merge is confirmed.
  • The spine fixes key concept, related concepts, global context and criteria. Content, texts and case studies stay with the teacher, so "same concepts, different content" is allowed in parallel classes.
  • The WWII and Holocaust core in MYP4 is what the LK20 aim on genocide and extremism (and the Germany-trip audit) would look for. Confirm with IT before trimming it.

What this map could not see
